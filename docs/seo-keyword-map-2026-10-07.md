## What currently ranking pages do well

- Dedicated Jaipur landing pages put the exact product and city in the title and H1.
- Strong product pages show price, pack size, delivery, method, reviews, and FAQs together.
- Rajasthan results emphasize origin, traditional method, and delivery coverage.
- India-level results are more competitive and commonly combine “buy online,” pack/price,
  verification or lab proof, customer reviews, and detailed product information.
- Search results reward pages that satisfy the query directly; repeating keywords without useful
  evidence is not a substitute for product detail, reviews, sourcing, and real business signals.
